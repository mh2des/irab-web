## Sending rules
One email per person per outlet. Their name, one sentence about their own article or page, the ask in one sentence, and something they can use without replying (screenshots, a Plus code, the teacher guide). Follow up once after six days, then stop. Send from press@irab.app or your personal address, never a no-reply. Log every send in `log.csv` (date, target, channel, template, reply, result).
