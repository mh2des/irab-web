# Outreach pitches for إعراب آب / Irab App

Rules that make these work: one recipient per email, their name, a reason that is about THEM (an article
they wrote, a resource list they maintain), the ask in one sentence, and something they can use without
replying (a fact, a screenshot, a free Plus code). Never ask for "a backlink"; ask for a review, a listing,
a quote, or a correction. Follow up once after 6 days, then stop. Send from mansoor@ or press@irab.app,
never a no-reply. Press kit: https://irab.app/press (AR) · https://irab.app/en/press (EN).

Subject lines are in the first line of each template. Replace {…} fields.
