## 300-word description (EN)
Irab App (إعراب آب) is built around one problem every Arabic learner meets: i'rab, the word-by-word grammatical analysis of a sentence. Textbooks explain the rules; exams test whether you can apply them to a sentence you have never seen. Irab App gives you both sides.

The analyzer: type, dictate or photograph any Arabic sentence and get, for every word, its part of speech, its syntactic role, its case, the case marker and the reason for it, plus full tashkeel. The engine runs at zero temperature and refuses its own output when the word count or vowelling does not match the sentence, so a wrong parse is never cached.

Practice: write your own i'rab for a sentence and get instant grading that points to the exact mistake, with a practice bank and seven challenge levels.

Lessons: 41 grammar lessons written and reviewed before publishing, each linked to Quran verses that use the construction.

Quran: word-by-word pages for every ayah, with a short original summary in Arabic or English and the classical reference credited on every page.

Teachers: live i'rab rooms (مجالس الإعراب) where a teacher hosts a quiz and students join from any browser, plus a printable one-page classroom guide.

Also included: a smart dictionary (meaning, root, pattern, conjugation, examples with tashkeel, translations) and a classical dictionary layer of 13 traditional works.

Built solo by Mansoor Hasan Shokla, a computer-science student in Malaysia and former Arabic instructor for non-native speakers. Free on the web with no sign-up for the first parses; Irab Plus unlocks unlimited analysis. iOS, Android and web share one account. Methodology and sources: https://irab.app/methodology
