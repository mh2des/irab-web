Data We Collect
البيانات التي نجمع

Account & Identity

  • Email address: provided during sign up
  • Display name: chosen by you, shown on leaderboards
  • Profile photo URL: optional, from Google if using Google Sign In
  • Firebase UID: anonymous internal identifier

Learning Progress

  • XP points, ranks, badges, streak records
  • Practice session results (accuracy, correct/incorrect counts)
  • Challenge history, power up counts, stage stars
  • Daily challenge attempts and scores
  • Saved favorites, Irab Library entries, Grammar Certificates

Usage & Analytics

  • Feature usage events (practice started, challenge completed, premium gate hit)
  • Daily usage counts per feature (for free tier limits)
  • Session metadata collected by Firebase Analytics (app version, OS, device type)
  • Product analytics and blurred session recordings collected by PostHog (EU, Frankfurt): screens visited, features used, subscription events, campaign source of the install (UTM parameters and ad click identifiers). Text you analyse, search or dictate is never sent to analytics; recordings mask all text and images. You can switch this off in the app Settings or via the notice on the website.
  • Crash reports and performance diagnostics

Search History

  • Arabic words and sentences submitted for irab/dictionary analysis
  • Timestamps of each query

Device & Technical

  • FCM push token (for notifications you opt into)
  • Firebase Installation ID and Android Device ID (for analytics)
  • IP address: processed by Cloudflare to deliver the service. For free use, your network address is also kept in short lived usage counters (see Free Use Limits below)

Free Use Limits (Abuse Prevention)

  • Daily usage counters: how many AI uses (analyses, dictionary lookups, questions and the like) an account, or a guest's anonymous ID, has made today: for the free limits and, for Irab Plus, a high ceiling against abuse
  • First use date: when an account first used an AI feature, to give it its welcome week
  • On the website (irab.app), for free use only: the same uses are also counted per browser and per network, so that one person gets one free allowance rather than one per account. For this the site sends us: a random browser ID that it keeps in your browser's local storage; a device signature, which is a one way hash of your browser type, operating system, language, screen size, time zone and processor and memory class, also kept in your browser (our server hashes it again together with your network); and your network address, meaning your IPv4 address or the first half (the /64 prefix) of your IPv6 address
  • Welcome week on the website: to give it once per browser and home network, we note which account each browser ID, device signature and home network saw first
  • Website guests: a guest's free tries are counted the same way, and also under the device signature over a longer period and per IP address and address range for the day
  • Email check: before a free website account uses AI features, we check that its email address is confirmed and not from a disposable email service

These identifiers are used only to enforce the free limits. They are never used for analytics or advertising, or to follow you on other sites, and the website's browser and network counts are not kept for Irab Plus subscribers.

Guest users: No Firestore profile is created for guests. In the app, a guest gets an anonymous ID: free tries are counted on the device and, for the day, on our server under that ID. On the website, guest tries are counted on the device and as described under Free Use Limits above.

How We Use Your Data
كيف نستخدم بياناتك

We use data solely to provide, maintain, and improve the Irab app:

  • Core service: Authenticate your account, process grammar analysis, store your progress, serve content
  • Gamification: Award XP, calculate rank, maintain streaks, display leaderboards
  • Personalization: Show saved favorites, library entries, practice history
  • Notifications: Send daily reminders and streak alerts (opt in only)
  • Subscription: Verify premium entitlement and gate features
  • Abuse prevention: Enforce free usage limits and request limits, including counting free website use per browser and network (see Section 02)
  • App improvement: Analyze aggregated, anonymized analytics to identify issues
  • Legal compliance: Maintain records required by law
We do not use your data for advertising, sell it to data brokers, or build ad profiles. Firebase Analytics data is used only for app improvement.

نستخدم البيانات حصرًا لتقديم تطبيق إعراب وصيانته وتحسينه:

لا نستخدم بياناتك للإعلانات ولا نبيعها لوسطاء البيانات. بيانات Firebase Analytics تُستخدم لتحسين التطبيق فقط.

AI Processing: Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude and OpenAI
معالجة الذكاء الاصطناعي: Google Gemini وAnthropic Claude وOpenAI
Important Disclosure: The Irab app uses Google Gemini AI: a third party AI service: for grammar analysis, dictionary, OCR, practice grading, and explanations. By using these features, you consent to your submitted content being processed by Google's Gemini API.

What Is Sent to Gemini

  • Arabic sentences submitted for irab analysis
  • Arabic words for dictionary lookups
  • Images (base64) when using OCR / camera
  • Sentences and user analysis during practice grading
  • Wrong words for explanation generation (premium)

How We Protect Your Data in AI Calls

  • All requests route through our Cloudflare Workers proxy: Gemini API keys are never exposed to your device
  • Firebase ID tokens are verified server side on every request
  • All communication is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS

What Gemini Does NOT Do

  • Does not use your text to train or improve AI models (per Google's API Terms)
  • Images sent for OCR are not stored after processing
  • We do not store raw images on our servers

See Google's privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy

Ask the Teacher: Anthropic Claude

The Ask the Teacher feature sends your question to Anthropic's Claude API, together with the sentence, its i'rab as shown in the app, the last few messages of the conversation and, when you ask from practice, your answer and the correct one. If Claude is unavailable, the same request is sent to Google Gemini instead. Anthropic does not use content sent through its API to train its models (per Anthropic's Commercial Terms). See Anthropic's privacy policy at anthropic.com/legal/privacy

If you rate a teacher's answer, your rating, the reasons you pick, any note you write, your question and the answer are stored on our Cloudflare servers with your account ID, so we can review and improve the teacher. When you ask the teacher to review a word's i'rab, the sentence, your objection and the teacher's ruling are stored the same way.

Smart Dictionary: OpenAI

Smart Dictionary entries are written by Google Gemini or OpenAI's API. Only the word you look up is sent. OpenAI does not use data sent through its API to train its models by default (per OpenAI's API data usage policy). See OpenAI's privacy policy at openai.com/policies/privacy-policy

إفصاح مهم: يستخدم تطبيق إعراب Google Gemini AI لتحليل الإعراب والقاموس والـOCR وتقييم التدرّب والشروح. باستخدامك هذه الميزات توافق على معالجة محتواك بواسطة Gemini API من Google.

ما الذي يُرسَل إلى Gemini

  • الجمل العربية للتحليل النحوي
  • الكلمات للبحث في القاموس
  • الصور (base64) عند استخدام OCR/الكاميرا
  • الجمل وتحليلاتك في جلسات التقييم
  • الكلمات الخاطئة لإنشاء الشروح (ميزة مميزة)

كيف نحمي بياناتك

  • جميع الطلبات تمرّ عبر وكيل Cloudflare Workers: لا تُكشف مفاتيح Gemini لجهازك
  • يُتحقَّق من رمز Firebase ID على الخادم في كل طلب
  • جميع الاتصالات مشفّرة بـHTTPS/TLS

ما لا يفعله Gemini ببياناتك

  • لا يستخدم نصوصك لتدريب نماذجه (وفق شروط Gemini API)
  • الصور المُرسَلة للـOCR لا تُخزَّن بعد المعالجة
  • لا نخزّن الصور الخام على خوادمنا

اسأل الأستاذ: Anthropic Claude

ترسل ميزة «اسأل الأستاذ» سؤالك إلى Claude API من Anthropic، ومعه الجملة وإعرابها كما يظهر في التطبيق، وآخر رسائل المحادثة، وإجابتك والإجابة الصحيحة إذا سألت من التدريب. وإذا تعذّر Claude أُرسل الطلب نفسه إلى Google Gemini. لا تستخدم Anthropic المحتوى المُرسَل عبر واجهتها البرمجية لتدريب نماذجها (وفق الشروط التجارية لـAnthropic). انظر سياسة خصوصية Anthropic: anthropic.com/legal/privacy

إذا قيّمت إجابة الأستاذ، تُخزَّن على خوادمنا في Cloudflare مع معرّف حسابك: تقييمك والأسباب التي تختارها وملاحظتك إن كتبتها وسؤالك والإجابة، لنراجعها ونحسّن الأستاذ. وحين تطلب من الأستاذ مراجعة إعراب كلمة، تُخزَّن الجملة واعتراضك وحكم الأستاذ بالطريقة نفسها.

Push Notifications
الإشعارات الفورية

With your explicit permission, we send push notifications via Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). We send two types:

  • Daily Practice Reminder: sent ~6 PM Gulf Time if you haven't practiced that day
  • Streak at Risk Alert: sent ~8 PM Gulf Time if your streak is at risk

You can revoke permission any time via device Settings → Notifications or from the app's Settings screen. On logout, your FCM token is removed from our servers. We do not send marketing push notifications.

بإذنك الصريح، نرسل إشعارات فورية عبر Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM). نرسل نوعين:

  • تذكير التدرّب اليومي: نحو الساعة ٦ مساءً بتوقيت الخليج إذا لم تتدرّب اليوم
  • تنبيه خطر انقطاع السلسلة: نحو الساعة ٨ مساءً إذا كانت سلسلتك في خطر

يمكنك إلغاء الإذن من إعدادات الجهاز أو شاشة إعدادات التطبيق. عند تسجيل الخروج يُحذف رمز FCM من خوادمنا. لا نرسل إشعارات تسويقية.

Subscriptions & Purchases
الاشتراكات والمشتريات

Irab offers optional premium subscriptions. All purchases are processed by Apple App Store (iOS) or Google Play (Android). We do not receive or store payment card details.

Subscription management is handled by RevenueCat, which receives your purchase receipt and User ID to verify entitlement. When you subscribe, RevenueCat notifies our server via webhook, updating your subscription status in Firestore. This status determines which premium features are unlocked.

Subscription status (tier, plan) is stored in Firestore linked to your account and deleted when you delete your account.

يوفر إعراب اشتراكات مميزة اختيارية. تُعالَج جميع المشتريات من خلال Apple App Store (iOS) أو Google Play (Android). لا نستلم بيانات بطاقتك ولا نخزّنها.

تُدار الاشتراكات بواسطة RevenueCat. عند اشتراكك تُخطر RevenueCat خادمنا لتحديث حالة اشتراكك في Firestore، وهي التي تحدّد الميزات المميزة المتاحة لك.

حالة الاشتراك مخزّنة في Firestore مرتبطة بحسابك وتُحذف عند حذف حسابك.

Data Retention
مدة الاحتفاظ بالبيانات
  • Account data (email, display name, profile): until account deletion
  • Learning progress, favorites, history: until account deletion
  • Daily usage counters (account, browser, network): expire after 2 days
  • First use date and website first account markers: 60 days
  • Website guest tries (device signature): 90 days; website guest address counters: 25 hours
  • XP award logs: auto deleted after 60 days
  • Certificates: until account deletion (stored in Cloudflare R2)
